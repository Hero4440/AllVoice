# LinkedIn Post — Kiro Spark Challenge Results

---

We won the Grand Prize ($4,500) AND Best Build ($1,500) — $6,000 total — at the Kiro Spark Challenge.

Out of 50 teams at Arizona State University, our team (Aman, Tejas, and Arnav) built AllVoice — a voice-controlled Chrome Extension copilot for blind and low-vision users. We chose the Ethics frame and built a six-stage pipeline with a mandatory Ethics Logic Gate — a pure function that blocks privacy violations before they reach the page. Passwords, payment fields, PII — if it's unsafe, the action never executes.

Best Build meant a lot because we went deep on Kiro's toolkit: a three-layer spec (requirements, design, tasks), five steering files, eight agent hooks automating everything from type checks to ethics gate integrity reviews, and MCP for live web access. 103 tests. All in one day.

One thing we're proud of: we documented the entire journey on LinkedIn as we built — not as an afterthought, but as part of the workflow. We used Kiro hooks to generate posts at key milestones, showing the community what spec-driven, ethical AI development looks like in practice.

The competition was incredible — teams built everything from economic simulators with Monte Carlo modeling to environmental and education tools. Taking the Grand Prize against that caliber of work means a lot.

This was my first hackathon ever. Walking in, I didn't know what to expect. Walking out with the Grand Prize and Best Build — $6,000 and two top awards — that's something I'll carry for a long time.

This experience reinforced something I believe deeply: accessibility, AI, and ethics aren't tradeoffs. They're layers of the same system. AllVoice proves that software can be powerful and principled at the same time.

Huge thanks to ASU Enterprise Technology, Kiro, Toptal, and all the mentors and judges — Brian Eisenlauer, Danny Kim, Paul Cioni, Jackee Kenny, Kofi Wood, Olivia Herneddo, and the entire organizing team. And to my teammates Tejas and Arnav — couldn't have done it without you.

The code is open source: github.com/Hero4440/AllVoice
